## Completed application and design reasoning

mello is a **localhost coursework skills prototype for fictional demonstrations**. Vue carries six cycles of four one-second inhale counts and six exhale counts. FastAPI/SQLite retain conversations, timestamps, session outcomes/events, explicit cue preferences and one shared local profile. Direct Start requires no question, disclosure, model response or questionnaire. Pause, immediate End and independently bundled Crisis support remain available. It does not provide therapy, diagnosis, clinical assessment or human monitoring, and has not been deployed as a support service.

Ready becomes a simpler exercise view based on the user's chosen activity, not detected distress. Animated/Text only can be overridden without changing the remembered choice. The persistent profile offers standard/brief replies and optional factual saved-practice context; defaults, reset, history deletion and explicit cue reuse preserve control. History distinguishes Completed, Stopped, Interrupted and Not started, with unknown timing shown honestly. Failed writes say Not saved and offer Retry or an unsaved exit.

The model selects an approved catalog topic; reviewed local rules apply explicit profile/history choices to the displayed response. The model receives the current question and approved facts, not raw profile/history, and cannot set pace, write preferences or navigate. Optional chatbot voice retains readable text and Play/Stop. Breathing is silent; words accompany phase colors, with text-only/reduced-motion, keyboard controls, contrast/text options and configurable screen-reader updates.

[Part 3.1 decisions revisited](https://github.com/abn54/Health-App/blob/92d55ad0cd8073795892ec7d35d783f53aa26151/docs/part3-revisions.md), [complete R1–R4/D1–D6 mapping and tradeoffs](https://github.com/abn54/Health-App/blob/92d55ad0cd8073795892ec7d35d783f53aa26151/docs/research-findings.md), [design/flow](https://github.com/abn54/Health-App/blob/92d55ad0cd8073795892ec7d35d783f53aa26151/docs/design.md), [architecture](https://github.com/abn54/Health-App/blob/92d55ad0cd8073795892ec7d35d783f53aa26151/docs/architecture.md), [README setup](https://github.com/abn54/Health-App/blob/92d55ad0cd8073795892ec7d35d783f53aa26151/README.md). One skill and optional questions reduce effort (D2/D3); the 60-second sequence addresses R1; independent support/exit address R2/R3/D4. Explicit personalization compromises between D5 convenience and R4 control. The [Kaggle resource](https://github.com/abn54/Health-App/blob/92d55ad0cd8073795892ec7d35d783f53aa26151/docs/dataset-design-notes.md) informed intent/pattern/approved-response organization through a limited preview; no dataset training, full-file audit or clinical-effect claim is made. No earlier travel/hotel source code was supplied or copied.

## Remaining limits and disclosure

**Untested:** real VoiceOver/NVDA use by a blind user. Voice quality and announcement lag depend on the device; selected automated/manual access checks are not WCAG certification. One active tab/shared unencrypted local profile, broader crash/cross-browser behavior, indirect phrase misses/negated phrase false positives, and broad model relevance remain limitations. Initial page loading requires the server. No clinical effectiveness, distress detector, peer feedback or safety guarantee is claimed.

[AI-USE.md](https://github.com/abn54/Health-App/blob/92d55ad0cd8073795892ec7d35d783f53aa26151/AI-USE.md) discloses Codex, tools/models and their roles.
